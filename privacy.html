TAJINKART Studio

Official Privacy Policy & Terms of Service • Effective Date: November 2026

Transparency Guarantee: TAJINKART Studio (directed by Seyed Soroush Taj) operates with strict data privacy standards. We collect minimal authentication information solely to manage member access, studio commissions, and portfolio interactions. We never sell or share user data.

1. Overview & Identity of the Studio

TAJINKART Studio (https://tajinkart.space) is an independent concept art and visual development atelier directed by Seyed Soroush Taj. This Privacy Policy governs user authentication, customer inquiries, digital assets acquisition, and client commissions conducted through the website.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information strictly necessary to provide authentic studio services:

  • Google Account Data (OAuth 2.0): When you choose to sign in with Google, we receive only basic public profile information: your name, primary email address, and profile picture avatar.
  • Direct Registration Data: If you register via email, we store your chosen display name, email address, password hash (salted and encrypted via Supabase Auth), and an optional contact phone number.
  • Order & Briefing Information: For commissioned concept art projects, we collect project specifications, reference materials, and billing communications.

3. How We Use Google User Data

In strict compliance with Google API Services User Data Policy:

  • Google user data is used exclusively for authenticating your identity and creating your member account in the studio portal.
  • We do not request sensitive or restricted scopes (such as Gmail, Drive, Contacts, or Calendar).
  • We do not sell, rent, or lease any Google user data to third parties, data brokers, or advertisers.
  • We do not use user data to train generalized artificial intelligence or machine learning models without express authorization.

4. Data Storage & Security Standards

All authentication records and profile details are secured using Supabase (an enterprise-grade PostgreSQL cloud platform with Row-Level Security, SSL/TLS encryption in transit, and AES-256 encryption at rest). Only authenticated requests authorized by security policies can access member data.

5. Cookies & Local Storage

Our website uses local storage and standard session cookies solely for session persistence (maintaining your logged-in state between visits) and language preferences. No tracking cookies are deployed.

6. User Rights & Data Deletion

Members retain full ownership and rights over their personal data. You may at any time:

  • Request a full export of your account details.
  • Request complete and permanent deletion of your profile, orders, and authentication data from our database.

To request data deletion or exercise your privacy rights, email the studio directly at tajinkart@gmail.com with the subject line "Data Deletion Request". Requests are fulfilled within 48 business hours.

7. Studio Terms of Service & Commissions

By engaging in commissions or purchasing digital assets, clients agree to the following operational policies:

  • Artistic Direction: Concept art deliverables, character sheets, and visual development pipelines are directed by Seyed Soroush Taj.
  • Payment Terms: Production begins following confirmation of initial briefing terms. Deliverable formats include high-resolution 300 DPI layered PSD master files and reference turnarounds.
  • Intellectual Property: Commercial usage rights and copyright transfers are governed by specific project agreements executed at the brief confirmation phase.
